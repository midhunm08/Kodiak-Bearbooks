# Bearbooks

**Free, offline-first accounting software. Your books live in your own files — not on someone's server.**

No account. No login. No subscription. No internet needed. Your data never leaves your device.

---

## How your data works (read this first)

This is the whole point of Bearbooks:

- **Windows:** everything you enter is saved in **one file — `accounts.db`** — inside the Bearbooks folder. Copy it to a pen drive and you have a full backup. Copy the whole folder to a new computer and your data moves with it.
- **Android:** your books are stored on your phone, saved in real time as you type. You choose where the data lives.
- Nothing is ever uploaded anywhere. There is no cloud account to create and no sync that can break.

## Download

Get them from the **Releases** page on the right side of this repo.

## Install — Android (2 minutes)

1. Download `bearbooks.apk` from Releases onto your phone.
2. Open the file. Your phone will ask permission to **"install unknown apps"** — allow it once for your browser/files app. (This is normal for apps installed outside the Play Store.)
3. Tap **Install**, then open Bearbooks.
4. That's it — start entering your accounts. Your data stays on your phone.

## Install — Windows (5 minutes)

1. Download `accounting-desktop.zip` from Releases and **unzip it** anywhere you like (e.g. your Documents folder).
2. If you don't have Python: go to [python.org/downloads](https://www.python.org/downloads/), install it, and on the very first screen **tick "Add python.exe to PATH"**. One-time only.
3. Open the unzipped folder and **double-click `start.bat`**.
4. Your browser opens Bearbooks automatically (at http://127.0.0.1:5000). If it doesn't, open that address yourself.
5. The first run installs what Bearbooks needs — wait a minute. Keep the black window open while you work; close it when you're done.

## Quick start — your first entries (same on both)

1. **Create ledgers:** open Accounts → add your Cash, Bank, and main expense/income ledgers. Start typing in any account field and matching ledgers appear as you type.
2. **Record a voucher:** open Vouchers → New → pick the type (Payment, Receipt, Sales, Purchase, Journal) → choose the ledgers, enter Dr/Cr amounts → Save.
3. **Check your books:** Dashboard shows your balances; Reports gives Trial Balance, P&L, and Balance Sheet; export any of them to real Excel (.xlsx) or PDF.

## Features

- Proper double-entry accounting with a two-column Dr/Cr trial balance
- Vouchers: payment, receipt, sales, purchase, returns, journal
- GST ledgers with simple inward/outward summaries
- Debtors & creditors with bill tracking and ageing
- Inventory, fixed assets with recurring depreciation, cost centres
- Budgets vs actuals, bank reconciliation, day-end tally
- Cash-flow forecast and period comparison dashboard
- Custom report builder and custom charts
- Bank statement import — you only choose which accounts to post to
- Quick Add screen for fast journal entries, duplicate-entry warning
- Autocomplete ledger picker (type 2+ letters, pick from matches)
- Real Excel (.xlsx) and paginated PDF exports, invoice PDF on entries
- Light / dark / system themes, accent colours, compact accounting layout

## Backup

- **Windows:** copy `accounts.db` anywhere — that's your entire books. (There's also a Backup page inside the app.)
- **Android:** use the in-app backup to save a copy of your data file.
